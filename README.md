## Make reading feel like yours

Librix helps you keep your reading life together. Build a personal library, pick up where you left off, and make steady progress at your own pace.

### Your library, at a glance

Add books with their author, category, cover, and reading status. Browse your collection on shelves arranged around the way you think about books.

### Pick up right where you stopped

Track pages and reading position as you read. Librix supports PDF and EPUB books, so your place is easier to find the next time you open a book.

### See the pages add up

Reading sessions, time spent, reading streaks, and progress help you notice the habit you are building—without turning reading into a race.

### A gentle nudge to read

Set a reading reminder on your device when it suits you. Choose a light, dark, or system-matched appearance, and use Librix in English, Portuguese, Spanish, or French.

### Look something up without leaving your reading space

The in-app web search lets you explore an author, a reference, or a new topic from inside Librix. Websites you visit are operated by their own providers and follow their own privacy practices.

## Your reading, on your device

Librix keeps profiles, books, progress, preferences, and reminders on your device. There is no Librix online-account service or cloud library sync. Librix does not encrypt its local data or exported backup files, so keep your device and backup files safe.

The last app update improve password protection, leave passwords out of new backups, and let you delete your local profile from inside Librix. These improvements are not available in an installed version until you install an app update that includes them. New backups from that update will not contain password data, and restoring one will ask you to create a new password. Older backups may contain a password in plain text; protect or delete those files separately. Deleting a Librix profile cannot erase backups or files saved outside the app.
